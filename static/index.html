What is Peacock Dating now?

A modern courtship format: men get up and tell the room who they are, what they care about, and what they are looking for. They can just talk, or show a few photos on a phone or iPad that reveal real hobbies, taste, adventure, work, projects, and attitude. Women listen, watch, check out the room, and privately decide who they want to know better.

The app-based version of dating is deadening people. Everyone hates the swipe, the profile theater, and the endless half-interest. This format puts people back in the room together so chemistry can happen the old way: through glances, looks, energy, style, confidence, timing, and the way someone carries themselves. Matches are still shared only on a mutual "yes."

Roles & Expectations

For Men

  • Be ready to stand up and speak for yourself.
  • Be brave. Share who you are, what you do, what you enjoy, and what matters to you.
  • Bring a few good photos on your phone or iPad if they help show your life, hobbies, or adventures.
  • Hosted interview format: you can decline any question.
  • Light jokes/banter from the host—stay good-humoured.
  • Your speaking time is rationed so everyone has a chance.

For Women

  • Come to listen, observe, and see which men interest you.
  • DM or pass questions to the host; they will ask them.
  • No pressure to speak unless you want to.
  • Listen comfortably, notice vibe and attitude, and note anyone you like.
  • After the event, submit picks privately. Mutual "yes" = intro.

For the Host

  • Keep speakers comfortable; surface personality.
  • Read selected questions from women and keep the energy moving.
  • Maintain pace, fairness, respect.
  • Remove anyone who is rude, intrusive, weird, or otherwise breaks the rules.

Eligibility, Rules & Safety

Community Guidelines

  • Ages 18+ only.
  • Sydney people only for now.
  • Respectful conduct; no harassment or doxxing.
  • No filming, screenshots, or recordings.
  • No contact details during event; only after mutual consent.
  • Questions may be skipped; men may decline.
  • No rude, intrusive, or weird behaviour. The host will moderate.
  • Hosts may remove rule-breakers.

FAQ

Is this like a dating app?⌄

No. It's a live, host-guided interview with clear rules and mutual-consent matching only.

Is this Sydney-only now?⌄

Yes. The group is being revived for Sydney people, with most events based around Town Hall.

Are events in person or remote?⌄

Mostly in person around Town Hall. We may still run occasional Sydney-only remote nights using the same host-guided format.

Do women have to speak?⌄

No. Women can mostly listen, observe, and pass questions through the host if they want.

How can women ask questions?⌄

In person, pass them through the host. On remote nights, DM them to the host. Men may skip any question.

How do matches work?⌄

After the event, women privately submit picks. We only introduce pairs that both say “yes.”

What should men prepare?⌄

A short intro, a story or two, and a clear sense of who you are. Bring a few good phone photos if they help show your hobbies, travel, projects, or style. Authenticity > performance.
